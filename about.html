OUR STORY

Visuals
come first.

That’s the idea behind PixtoLearn. A picture can help someone see what comes next, recognise something familiar or understand how to take part.

BUILT AROUND REAL LIFE

At home.
In a session.
In the water.

We make visual tools for families and the professionals who work with them: therapists, occupational therapists, swimming coaches and PE teachers.

Designed for a wide range of learners, with particular attention to SEND and neurodivergent people.

One idea. Different ways to use it.

Our app brings together Routines, Swimming, Physical Activity and My Voice. Our physical Swimming packs take visual support into the water.
