Ten ways to hold the session.

The line under the title is gone. Each study uses the same eight pairs, in a tighter frame, with a different way to move through them.

01

The row

Every line is already on the page. The picture arrives at the end of the one you choose.

02

The fold

The picture opens under the line, only as tall as it needs. The next choice closes it.

03

The turn

One card. The problem is the front. Turn it, and the answer and the picture are the back.

04

The gutter

The problem and the answer face each other. The picture is a slim mark between them.

05

The index

Eight numbers. One low bar holds the answer and the picture. Nothing taller than the line.

06

On the picture

The words sit on the photograph. The eight answers are chips under the frame.

07

In the sentence

The answer takes the place of the struck words. The picture is a stamp in the line.

08

The cells

Eight cells, and no empty stage. Press one and the picture fills that cell.

09

The strip

A film you slide. Each frame is the picture and the answer, side by side in one band.

10

The result holds it

The ending is the frame. Each answer is a chip, and its picture sits on the chip.
