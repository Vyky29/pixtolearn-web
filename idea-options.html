Twenty photos

The drawing on the iPad is a file we already have in assets/idea, pasted in, not redrawn. It is the same picto as on the website: flat colour, one black outline, white card. If that picto shows a giraffe, the photograph has giraffes. If it shows a blue suitcase, the photograph has that suitcase.

The iPad is copied from a real photograph of someone holding one. Both hands cradle the bottom edge, thumbs on the front corners, screen facing the camera as a flat rectangle. An 11-inch iPad is about as wide as the person’s torso. Mode 1 is portrait. Mode 1-2 and 1-2-3 are landscape.

Shots with no file in assets/idea are not generated. We do not invent a child to fill the card.
