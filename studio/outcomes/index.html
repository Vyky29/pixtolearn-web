Why centres care

The pack stops being a product and becomes a syllabus tool

Teachers do not need a new scheme. They need proof that the cards they order will support the scheme they already run.

Same SKU, two markets

Leisure operators on Swim England and international or independent centres on STA buy the identical Full Pack. One inventory line, two pitch decks.

Hardest outcomes become strips

Shark survival chains and Stage 7 sequences stop being spoken paragraphs. They become four to seven images on a keyring a learner can rehearse on poolside.

Break it down is the closer

Flip any skill card in this document and the teaching steps appear. The buyer sees both the learner cue and the teacher cue without leaving the page.
