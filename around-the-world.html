PixtoLearn Around the World

Helping make water safety accessible for every child

We start small: one resource pack and one community at a time.

Support the project See Mission 1

Around the world, many children grow up near rivers, lakes and coastlines without access to structured water safety education.

Our goal is simple

Create free visual water safety resources

Share them with schools, charities and community organisations supporting children in underserved communities.

Together, we believe visual learning can help save lives.

Mission 1

Equip one school with free visual water safety resources

Not a hundred countries. Not a vague global promise. One clear, reachable mission people can follow.

First goal

Fund the first 100 free visual water safety resources

These resources will be free for selected schools and organisations working with children where access to educational materials is limited.

Where we hope to help

Trusted local partners, one community at a time

As the project grows, we want to work with local organisations where drowning risk is high, including parts of:

  • Sub-Saharan Africa
  • Bangladesh
  • India
  • South-East Asia

Every project will be developed with local partners so resources stay relevant, respectful and practical.
