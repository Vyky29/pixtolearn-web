PixtoLearn Around the World

Help children in India learn water safety through visual learning

One visual pack. Real impact. Safer futures.

Sponsor impact See the India Pilot

Every year, thousands of children in India are exposed to water without structured swimming or water safety education. In many communities, teachers and facilitators want to help but lack training, tools, and clear guidance.

PixtoLearn Swimming was created to change that.

The problem

Safety cannot wait for perfect conditions

  • Drowning is one of the leading causes of accidental death in children worldwide
  • Many schools and community programmes do not have trained swimming teachers
  • Traditional training relies heavily on language, theory, and experience
  • Children are left without safe, consistent guidance around water

Our solution

Clear visuals. Safer teaching. Less dependence on words.

PixtoLearn Swimming breaks water safety and basic aquatic skills into clear, simple, step-by-step visuals.

Non-specialist facilitators

Local facilitators can teach more safely with a shared visual method.

Less reliance on words

Children understand what is expected without depending on language alone.

Works with limited resources

Programmes can run consistently, even where specialist coaches are scarce.

The India Pilot Programme

Fund the first PixtoLearn Swimming pilot in India

This campaign funds a real pilot, not a vague global promise. India is the beginning of PixtoLearn Swimming Global Impact.

What we will deliver

A focused 6 to 8 week water safety programme

This is not a swimming stroke programme. It is about confidence, safety, floating, breathing, and basic movement in water.

1 pilot start
  • 30 to 60 children supported
  • 3 to 6 local facilitators trained
  • Visual packs used directly in sessions
  • Full documentation and impact reporting

Each sponsored pack is used directly in the pilot. It is not shipped to supporters.
