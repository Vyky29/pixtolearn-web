The project

A visual water safety system that can travel

Around the world, many children grow up near rivers, lakes and coastlines without access to structured water safety education. Teachers and facilitators often want to help, but lack tools, training, and a clear method.

Our goal

Create free visual water safety resources that local partners can use

Share them with schools, charities and community organisations supporting children in underserved communities.

We start small: one mission, one community, one clear outcome at a time.

The problem

Safety cannot wait for perfect conditions

  • Drowning is one of the leading causes of accidental death in children worldwide
  • Many schools and community programmes do not have trained swimming teachers
  • Traditional training relies heavily on language, theory, and experience
  • Children are left without safe, consistent guidance around water

Our solution

Clear visuals. Safer teaching. Less dependence on words.

PixtoLearn Swimming breaks water safety and basic aquatic skills into clear, simple, step-by-step visuals.

Non-specialist facilitators

Local facilitators can teach more safely with a shared visual method.

Less reliance on words

Children understand what is expected without depending on language alone.

Works with limited resources

Programmes can run consistently, even where specialist coaches are scarce.

This is not a swimming stroke programme. It is about confidence, safety, floating, breathing, and basic movement in water.
