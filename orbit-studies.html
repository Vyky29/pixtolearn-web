Ten ways around the water.

The three packs float in a row, in the middle, with no box around them. People travel the ring outside them. Choose someone, and the note opens beside the water: the packs we recommend, and why.

01

Near water

A low pool. The packs float in a row in the middle. Whoever comes down toward you gets larger, and never crosses the products.

02

The table

The ring lies a little flat, like the near edge of a round table. The products float in the middle, with nothing around them.

03

Soft focus

A fuller circle. The one behind falls soft and small. The one in front stays sharp, and the packs in the middle do not move.

04

Two tides

A second ring, and a little current on the path. The products hold the centre while the water moves.

05

The lens

A thick glass disc. Portraits ride the rim. The packs float under the lens, still, in a row.

06

The bow

Water to the horizon. The path is a shallow bow around the packs, not a line through them.

07

Hairline

One blue line and three circles. The packs have no plate. The space around them is the quiet.

08

The well

Deeper water at the rim, light in the middle. The packs rest in that light. People circle the edge.

09

The pass

Wide and low. One portrait crosses large in front of the packs. The others stay small and soft behind.

10

Beside you

The same water. The packs float in a row. When you choose, the note opens beside it: the packs, and the reason for each one.
