THE IDEA BEHIND PIXTOLEARN

Why do visuals
speak louder?

Words pass. A picture stays.
Something to look at, come back to and follow — at your own pace.

ANTICIPATE · Hairdresser

Helps you see what’s coming.

Looking at arrival and the cape beforehand helps make the visit more predictable. The pictures stay available to look at together.

ANTICIPATE · Dentist

See the visit before you go.

Sit in the chair. Open your mouth. Make the next part of the visit visible.

ANTICIPATE · Blood test

Know what happens next.

Use the pictures to talk through sitting down and putting the band around the arm.

RECOGNISE · At home

Familiar things, easy to find.

Soap and a toothbrush: connect the picture with the real object in front of you.

RECOGNISE · At the pool

See it. Find it. Use it.

Match the ball and the pool noodle to their pictures, ready for the next activity.

RECOGNISE · Getting ready

Make the equipment familiar.

Recognise the goggles and the hoop before using them in the water.

UNDERSTAND · Routines

One instruction at a time.

At the dentist: first sit in the chair, then open your mouth. Two pictures make the sequence clear.

UNDERSTAND · Swimming

Makes the next action clearer.

Three pictures show how to blow bubbles: breathe in, put your face in the water, then blow. Each step gives you something specific to follow.

UNDERSTAND · Physical Activity

See how the movement happens.

Walk forward: look forward, take a step, keep going. Every step has its picture and its words.
