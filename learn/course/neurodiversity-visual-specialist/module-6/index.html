Key ideas

1

Read early signs

Body, voice, withdrawal and refusal are information.

2

Known visuals only

Break, finished and calm routines beat new skill strips in escalation.

3

Re-enter gently

Shorten the demand, return to first-then, celebrate recovery without shame.
