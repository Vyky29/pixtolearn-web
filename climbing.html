    The session problem

    When the plan is only spoken, the room loses the thread

    Climbing rooms move fast. Holds, colours, safety and turn-taking collide. Without a visible plan, anxious climbers freeze and coaches fill the gap with more words.

    At the wall

    Overload

    Too many holds, too many instructions. Learners need one clear next action.

    Safety & prep

    Missed steps

    Harness, helmet, wait, go — if the order lives only in speech, steps get skipped.

    Between climbs

    Hard wait times

    Without a visible First / Next / Then, waiting spikes anxiety.
