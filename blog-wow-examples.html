Using WOW cards

7 Aug 2026

← Back to Resources
7 Aug 2026 · PixtoLearn

WOW is a simple system: Words name the moment, Objects show what you need, and Ways show how to do it. Once you see it in one routine, you can spot it everywhere.

Here are six everyday moments built the same way.

Brushing teeth

Start with a Word that sets intent, then the Object in hand, then the Way that turns it into action.

Getting dressed

Clothes make this especially clear: the Object is the item; the Way is putting it on.

Climbing ready

The same pattern works outside the bathroom — kit becomes action when Ways sit next to Objects.

Shower

Care routines stay calm when the Object and the Way are side by side.

Going outside

Leaving the house is often a hard transition — one clear Object–Way pair helps it land.

Washing hair

Same bathroom, different moment — shampoo as Object, the squeeze as Way.

How to use this

Pick a moment your learner finds hard. Lay out one Word, one Object, and one Way. Keep the sequence short. Repeat it until the picture feels familiar — then add the next step.
