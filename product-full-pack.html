Flashcards & Learning Resources

  • 160 waterproof flashcards, including indexes and instructions

  • Folder 3: Swimming Strokes, Diving, Turns & Starts
  • Folder 4: Games, Toys & Equipment, Extras

  • 1 Session Plan Folder to support structured and flexible lesson planning

Poolside Tools & Accessories

  • Swimming Stands & Holder (white bag): 3 stands (First, Next, Then) and a holder for clear poolside routines
