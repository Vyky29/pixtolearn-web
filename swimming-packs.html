SCHOOLS & ORGANISATIONS

One set for every class.

A class needs the same pictures every time. Two packs cover that.

BasicRecommendedThe skill cards on their own, when the toys stay out of the lesson. FullRecommendedThe case for every class. Cards, toys, and stands.
COACHES

The session decides.

The water changes from one lesson to the next. A coach can bring any of the three.

BasicRecommendedThe steps, held at the side of the pool. FunRecommendedThe yellow bag, when the skill becomes a game. FullRecommendedThe whole case, when one session uses all of it.
