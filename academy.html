Why we built Academy

Because tools alone are not enough. People need a shared method.

PixtoLearn exists for the moments when words fail: overload, freeze, shutdown, refusal, confusion. Academy trains the adults who support learners of every age: children, teens, adults with learning difficulties, and older adults living with dementia. Packs, WOW and the App become one calm language, not three separate habits.

  • For you: confidence, a clear method, and practice you can use the same day.
  • For them: less uncertainty and more dignity for children, teens, adults and people living with dementia.
  • For teams: one visual system across home, school, day services, residential care, therapy and pool.

The problem

Busy places make spoken teaching hard to follow.

Pools are noisy. Homes are rushed. Classrooms, day services and care settings compete for attention. Long verbal instructions often fade before a learner can act.

Academy answers each one with the same method: Open to Check, then Packs, WOW and App as one language.

  • Multi-step talk disappears mid-skill
  • Transitions trigger anxiety and refusal
  • Staff use different methods every session
  • Learners who process visually get left behind
