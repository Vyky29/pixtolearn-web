Naming governance: one pack per sport

Updated 14 Aug 2026. The Prep/Activity split was reversed to reduce portal clutter.

Model

  • sports: one pack per sport. Kit/arrive/ready then warm-up through finish/exit in one sequence.
  • travel: day destination / spectator (not playing).
  • skill decks: separate product; never -prep or -wow.

IDs

  • Pattern: {sport} (climbing, swimming, football, ...)
  • Forbidden for new packs: -wow, {sport}-prep, {sport}-activity
