Schedules

Fourth app pillar. Visual day board (TEACCH-style). Not the routine Steps player.

Job

Show what happens in the day (home / school). Learner sees a vertical strip of square PECS tiles, marks NOW, moves finished tiles aside.

Not for: City sightseeing as primary product (that lives in Routines / Create my routine).
Not for: Fine motor steps of brushing (that is Steps inside a pack).

  • Tiles: square PECS, label under image
  • Board: vertical top to bottom

  • Finished: move to Done pile / strip (side or bottom)
  • Now-Next mode: same UI, only 2 slots
  • First/Next/Then and Focus landscape stay inside Routines (horizontal OK there)

Create and save (account)

  1. Open Schedules → Create
  2. Name (Morning, School day, Therapy…)
  3. Add tiles from symbol palette
  4. Reorder drag; remove; Save
  5. Saved schedules stay on the account (fixed templates the family reuses)
