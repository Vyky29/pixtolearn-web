    The session problem

    When the plan is only spoken, the room loses the thread

    Yoga classes often depend on long verbal scripts. When attention or language is limited, the mat becomes confusing. PixtoLearn Yoga makes the sequence visible.

    Verbal overload

    Long pose names and continuous talk lose learners who need one clear picture.

    When everyone moves at different speeds, a visible board keeps the room together.
